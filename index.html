Build Your Own
Space Craft.

In this work, the goal is to provide you with an instruction manual documenting how to build a craft that is designed to operate in the air, water and land (along with options to source its production).

Purpose

  • minimize our suffering.
  • get us off of land and into the water or air to eleviate many of our problems caused by the invisible world around us.
  • provide affordable housing, transportation and food.
  • provide a comfortable and elegant liveable structure that can function on ground, air and water.
  • provide low-to-no cost transportation using an energy that is under utilized.
  • provide a source of sustainable food growing (with wheat and grapes {for bread and wine] taking precedence for their importance as shown to us by our savior Jesus Christ).
  • help those suffering escape many of their problems by getting off the ground and away from the source of their problems, as the Bible has made clear to us.
  • create awareness about the true nature of life on earth.
  • create awareness about the significance of Jesus Christ and the Holy Bible and His and its incredible importance to life on earth.

Design

  • roughly 4900 square feet; 70x70.
  • an interior courtyard.
  • indoor and courtyard food growing.
  • seven customizable living zones: living, bedroom, kitchen, prayer, gym, art/music, control.
  • bathroom and shower in the courtyard.
  • make assembly as easy as possible.
  • try to keep build costs under seventy thousand US (70K; target 36k).
  • marine desalinator for water supply.
  • intended to fly at low altitudes (25-70ft) and over water (in coastal regions) to avoid air/land conflict, though cloud-level may be ultimately be required for best results (per the Bible).
  • flight speed is not an inital priority, this is not the Indy 500.
  • minimize electrical energies for defensive purposes.
  • zero digital technology goal.
  • harness a low-to-no cost energy for flight.

Operations

  • non-profit or public benefit corporation.
  • decentralized autonomous organization.
  • open source everything not dangerous.
  • use open source software as an operational model.
  • communities, themes, marketplaces, etc.

Timeline

  • dec 2028: alpha verison 1 created.
  • dec 2029: production plans distributed.
